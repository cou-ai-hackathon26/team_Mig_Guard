# Official Hackathon Final Submission Text (150 Words Max)

**Project Title:** Mig_Guard — AI-Powered Pre-Departure Job Offer Checker for Migrant Workers  
**Domains:** Cybersecurity (Scam & Fraud Prevention) | Accessibility (Low-Literacy Bangla Voice)

---

### Submission Text (Word Count: 142 words)

Over 75% of exploited Bangladeshi migrant workers receive no formal contract before departure, and 80% are recruited via informal sub-agents, leaving hundreds of thousands vulnerable to human trafficking and cyber-scam compounds. Existing interventions act only after exploitation occurs. 

Mig_Guard is an explainable, pre-departure verification web app. Workers photograph an offer letter or recruiter chat screenshot on WhatsApp/Telegram and receive an instant, audio-read risk verdict in everyday Bangla. 

Rather than a simple prompt wrapper, Mig_Guard combines multimodal vision extraction with 15 deterministic legal rules, checking offers against official BMET recruiting licenses, statutory fee caps, and wage benchmarks. It highlights verbatim document evidence, flags missing clauses, generates crucial questions to ask recruiters, and provides emergency helplines. 

With zero document storage, privacy-first PII masking, and a 100% recall evaluation on scam indicators, Mig_Guard empowers workers before money is paid.
