# Recruitment Fees and Payments

## Request an Itemized Cost Statement
- **Rule:** Ask for a written list of recruitment, training, medical, visa, insurance, transport, and accommodation charges.
- **Risk:** A lump-sum or unexplained demand makes hidden costs and overpayment difficult to challenge.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).

## Pay Through Traceable Channels
- **Rule:** Use documented payment methods and retain receipts showing the recipient, purpose, date, and amount.
- **Risk:** Cash or personal mobile-wallet payments are difficult to trace if the recruiter disappears or changes the offer.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).

## Beware of Pressure to Pay Immediately
- **Rule:** Pause before paying when a recruiter demands money "today," threatens cancellation, or refuses normal verification.
- **Risk:** Artificial urgency is commonly used to prevent workers from checking fees, employers, or visa approval.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).

## Do Not Pay for an Unissued Visa
- **Rule:** Confirm the visa application or approval status and obtain written evidence before large payments.
- **Risk:** Paying before a visa or employer authorization exists increases the chance of non-refund and departure failure.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Salary Deductions Must Be Written
- **Rule:** The contract should state any permitted deductions, their purpose, amount, and duration.
- **Risk:** Undisclosed deductions can reduce wages below the promised amount or continue after the worker has repaid recruitment costs.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).
