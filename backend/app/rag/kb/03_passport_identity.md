# Passport and Identity Safety

## Employer or Recruiter Holding a Passport
- **Rule:** Keep possession of the passport unless a verified authority requires temporary custody under a documented process.
- **Risk:** Passport retention can restrict movement, isolate the worker, and enable coercion or forced labour.
- **Source:** ILO Forced Labour, Modern Slavery and Trafficking in Persons (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/forced-labour-modern-slavery-and-trafficking-persons, 2026-11-23).

## Request Document Custody Records
- **Rule:** If an authority temporarily holds a document, obtain written authorization, a receipt, the holder's details, and a return date.
- **Risk:** Informal custody makes it difficult to recover documents or prove that the worker authorized the transfer.
- **Source:** ILO Forced Labour and Human Trafficking (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/forced-labour-modern-slavery-and-trafficking-persons, 2026-11-23).

## Verify the Recruiting Agent
- **Rule:** Check the agent's registration, license number, office address, owner or responsible officer, and official complaint channel.
- **Risk:** An unlicensed or impersonated recruiter may collect fees and disappear, or provide false job and visa documents.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).

## Protect Identity Documents
- **Rule:** Share passport, national-ID, and certificate copies only through verified official channels and keep clean copies for the worker.
- **Risk:** Unknown recipients may use personal documents for identity theft, unrelated employment, or fraudulent visa applications.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Avoid Payment to an Individual
- **Rule:** Confirm the employer or registered agency's official payment instructions before transferring recruitment or training funds.
- **Risk:** Payments to personal accounts may be untraceable and may not belong to the company listed in the offer.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).
