# Verifying Fees and Recruiting Agencies

## Verifying a Recruiting Agency (RL Number)
- **Rule:** Only recruiting agencies with a valid Recruiting License (RL) issued by the Bureau of Manpower, Employment and Training (BMET) may legally recruit workers from Bangladesh.
- **Risk:** Unregistered sub-agents (dalals) have no legal accountability if the job turns out to be fake.
- **Source:** BMET Official Portal (Verified: https://bmet.gov.bd/, 2026-11-24).

## Official Migration Cost Caps and Payment Receipts
- **Rule:** Workers must verify applicable government migration costs for their destination and retain written evidence of all charges and payments.
- **Risk:** Cash payments or transfers to personal mobile-money accounts leave no reliable legal proof of payment.
- **Source:** Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment (Verified: https://probashi.gov.bd/, 2026-11-24).

## Pre-Departure Smart Card and BMET Clearance
- **Rule:** Before departing Bangladesh for work, workers should obtain the BMET emigration clearance (Smart Card) and complete required pre-departure procedures.
- **Risk:** Traveling without BMET clearance can prevent access to official welfare, insurance, and repatriation support.
- **Source:** BMET Pre-Departure Guidance (Verified: https://bmet.gov.bd/, 2026-11-24).
