# Overseas Employment Contract Checklist

## Essential Clauses in a Valid Overseas Contract
- **Rule:** A complete employment contract must clearly state the employer's legal name and address, job title, basic monthly salary and currency, working hours, overtime rate, contract duration, accommodation, food and medical coverage, and return air ticket.
- **Risk:** Leaving critical clauses blank or vague allows employers to change conditions after arrival.
- **Source:** BMET Employment and Pre-Departure Guidance (Verified: https://bmet.gov.bd/, 2026-11-24).

## Contract Substitution Risk (Signing on Arrival)
- **Rule:** Never travel abroad on a promise that the real employment contract will be signed after arriving at the destination airport.
- **Risk:** Signing a replacement contract after departure removes pre-departure proof of the agreed pay and job terms and reduces the worker's leverage.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment and Labour Migration Guidance (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-24).
