# Working Terms and Conditions

## Record Salary and Pay Cycle
- **Rule:** The contract must state the gross salary, currency, deductions, payment date, overtime rate, and account or method of payment.
- **Risk:** A later decision or verbal promise leaves the worker unable to verify the agreed pay.
- **Source:** ILO International Labour Standards and Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration/international-labour-standards-and-labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Confirm Working Hours and Rest
- **Rule:** Verify hours per day, days per week, overtime rules, leave, holidays, and rest arrangements before departure.
- **Risk:** Undefined terms can lead to unpaid or unsafe working conditions that differ from the offer.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Verify Accommodation and Transport
- **Rule:** Ask whether accommodation, food, utilities, and work transport are included and who pays for each.
- **Risk:** Deductions or unsuitable housing can consume most of the salary or expose the worker to unsafe conditions.
- **Source:** ILO International Labour Standards and Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration/international-labour-standards-and-labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Reject Unlimited Employer Changes
- **Rule:** Do not accept a contract that lets the employer change job duties, location, or salary without consent or due process.
- **Risk:** Broad clauses can permit contract substitution or assignment to work that was never agreed.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).

## Confirm Termination and Return Terms
- **Rule:** The contract should explain notice periods, repatriation or return-ticket responsibility, and what happens if the employer cancels.
- **Risk:** Missing terms can leave the worker responsible for travel costs or stranded after job cancellation.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).
