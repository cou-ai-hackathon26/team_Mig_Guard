# Scam Patterns and Migrant Worker Rights

## Cyber-Scam Compound Trafficking Patterns
- **Rule:** Job advertisements offering high-paying online customer service, cryptocurrency, or data-entry work in Southeast Asian border areas without formal qualifications are a major trafficking indicator.
- **Risk:** Victims may be lured with tourist visas, have their passports confiscated, and be forced to work in online fraud compounds.
- **Source:** UNODC / Southeast Asia Organized Crime Threat Assessment (Verified: https://www.unodc.org/roseap/en/2026/07/south-east-asia-interconnected-criminal-economy/story.html, 2026-11-24).

## Passport Ownership and Freedom of Movement
- **Rule:** Workers must retain possession of their own passports and must not surrender them to an employer or recruiter.
- **Risk:** Confiscating a worker's passport restricts freedom of movement and prevents the worker from leaving an abusive workplace or contacting embassy and repatriation support.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment Principles (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-24).

## Debt Bondage and Illegal Salary Deductions
- **Rule:** Employers and recruiters must not require workers to repay visa, airfare, or recruitment fees through undisclosed or arbitrary salary deductions.
- **Risk:** Illegal deductions can trap workers in debt bondage and prevent them from leaving or affording return travel.
- **Source:** ILO General Principles and Operational Guidelines for Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/publications/general-principles-and-operational-guidelines-fair-recruitment, 2026-11-24).
