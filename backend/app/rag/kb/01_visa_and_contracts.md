# Visa and Contracts

## Work Visa vs. Visit/Tourist Visa
- **Rule:** A worker must travel only with the work visa or permit that authorizes the offered employment.
- **Risk:** Working under a tourist or visit visa can leave the worker undocumented and exposed to arrest, deportation, exploitation, or loss of legal protections.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Mandatory Written Employment Contract
- **Rule:** Obtain a complete written employment contract before departure, signed by both parties and available in a language the worker understands.
- **Risk:** Verbal promises or chat messages may be unenforceable, while terms added after arrival can differ substantially from the promised job.
- **Source:** ILO International Labour Standards and Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration/international-labour-standards-and-labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Match All Contract Details
- **Rule:** Verify that the employer, job title, workplace, salary, currency, working hours, and contract duration match the offer and visa documents.
- **Risk:** Inconsistencies may indicate contract substitution, a fake employer, or an unauthorized job category.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Contract in an Understandable Language
- **Rule:** Do not sign if the worker does not understand the language, terms, or attachments. Obtain an accurate translation or independent explanation.
- **Risk:** Signing under confusion or pressure can waive rights and leave the worker bound to unfair terms.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Never Sign a Blank Contract
- **Rule:** Do not sign blank, incomplete, undated, or unsigned copies of a contract.
- **Risk:** Missing sections can be filled later with lower pay, a changed job, or additional deductions.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).
