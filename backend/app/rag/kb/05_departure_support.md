# Departure and Support

## Verify the Offer Before Travel Purchases
- **Rule:** Confirm visa approval, employer identity, flight arrangements, insurance, accommodation, and contract status before buying non-refundable travel items.
- **Risk:** The worker may lose money or travel under the wrong visa if the offer is cancelled or falsified.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Keep a Copy of Every Document
- **Rule:** Retain the signed contract, visa, offer, receipts, tickets, insurance, medical records, and message history.
- **Risk:** Missing evidence makes disputes and fraud reports harder to resolve.
- **Source:** ILO Fair Recruitment (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/fair-recruitment, 2026-11-23).

## Share Itinerary and Contact Details
- **Rule:** Tell a trusted person the employer address, flight details, contract terms, and local emergency contacts.
- **Risk:** Isolation makes it harder to report danger or obtain help after arrival.
- **Source:** ILO Labour Migration (Verified: https://www.ilo.org/topics-and-sectors/labour-migration, 2026-11-23).

## Use Bangladesh's Emergency Service
- **Rule:** In Bangladesh, call 999 for an emergency requiring police, fire, or ambulance assistance.
- **Risk:** Delayed help can worsen a threat to health or safety.
- **Source:** Bangladesh Police Hot Line Numbers (Verified: https://www.police.gov.bd/en/hot_line_number, 2026-11-23).

## Contact the Expatriates' Welfare Ministry
- **Rule:** For migration-related government inquiries, contact the Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment at +880-2-41030444.
- **Risk:** Using unofficial agents or addresses may prevent access to the correct complaint or verification process.
- **Source:** Ministry of Expatriates' Welfare and Overseas Employment (Verified: https://probashi.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/694032ef35ce18e1c05646cf, 2026-11-23).
